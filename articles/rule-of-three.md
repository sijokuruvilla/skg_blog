# Rule of three

Apply a rule of three to execution. Make it your default focussing question.

What are the 3 outcomes to make progress on:

1. Today
2. This week
3. This month

Our brains work in interesting ways. Upto 3 to 5 things we can recall from memory with relative ease. Beyond 5, recall performance degrades. Sweet spot being 3.

Work with three. Limit to three.

The clarity of thought will drive the quality of your execution.
